The rules

Based on the "feels like" temperature for the coldest hour you are out, adjusted by your settings. Cycling feels about 2°C colder from the wind you make yourself. By car, only the walk to the car counts, so it is 2°C kinder.

24° or moreT-shirt
18 to 24°T-shirt, a light layer to carry
12 to 18°Long sleeves and a light jacket
6 to 12°Sweater and a jacket
0 to 6°Sweater and a warm coat
Below 0°Winter coat, hat, gloves and scarf
Rain 50% or more (40% on a bike)Umbrella, or rain jacket and trousers on a bike
Rain 30 to 50%Pack an umbrella just in case
SnowWaterproof boots
Wind 30 km/h or moreWindproof outer layer
Below 8° on a bikeGloves
UV 3 or moreSunglasses
UV 6 or moreSunscreen
